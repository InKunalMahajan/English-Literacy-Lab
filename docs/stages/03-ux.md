# Stage 3 — UI/UX and Information Architecture (ELL-UX-003)

**Status:** Conceptual design draft; no high-fidelity or usability approval.

## Workspaces
Public website; Learner (`/learn`); Teacher (`/teach`); Curriculum Developer (`/curriculum-workspace`); Researcher (`/research-workspace`); School Admin (`/school`); Organisation Admin (`/organisation`); Platform Admin (`/platform`).

## Experience
Accessible story reader with visible ISL signing and English text; reading and comprehension; independent writing and recoverable drafts; teacher rubric feedback; separate ISL/English measures; governed research dashboards.

## Visual baseline
Minimal black-and-white design, restrained semantic colours, accessible focus/error states, responsive layouts from 320px, readable English text, no audio-only workflows.

## Components
Forms, inputs, navigation, cards, data tables, charts with tabular alternatives, ISL video player, story reader, writing editor, rubric scorer, research filters, alerts, loading and empty states.

## Acceptance
Keyboard operability, WCAG 2.2 AA target, Deaf-user review, safe draft recovery, private data separation, accessible consent and research reporting.

**Traceability:** ILL-UX-003 → ELL-UX-003.
