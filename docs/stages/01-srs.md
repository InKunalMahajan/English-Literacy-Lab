# Stage 1 — Software Requirements (ELL-SRS-001)

**Status:** Drafted in planning discussions; formal approval pending.

## Scope
Independent, multi-institution ISL-first English literacy learning, instruction, assessment and educational research. Initial FYJC focus, extensible to other levels. Separate ISL comprehension and English literacy outcomes.

## Roles (11)
Platform Owner; Platform Content Manager; Organisation Administrator; School Administrator; Teacher; Learner; Curriculum Developer; Research Principal Investigator; Research Assistant; Accessibility Reviewer; Guardian.

## Functional modules (24)
M01 Authentication & Account Security; M02 Organisations & Schools; M03 Roles & Permissions; M04 Users & Enrolments; M05 Curriculum Management; M06 Storybook Library; M07 ISL & Visual Resources; M08 Vocabulary & Grammar; M09 Reading Workspace; M10 Comprehension Activities; M11 Writing Workspace; M12 Assignments & Scheduling; M13 Learner Portfolio; M14 Baseline & Diagnostic Assessment; M15 Rubrics & Teacher Feedback; M16 Instructional Supports; M17 Progress Monitoring; M18 Research Studies; M19 Consent & Research Eligibility; M20 Research Data Collection; M21 Research Analytics; M22 Reports & Exports; M23 Notifications & Audit Logs; M24 Privacy, Retention & Governance.

## Nonfunctional requirements
Tenant isolation, secure access, WCAG 2.2 AA, keyboard access, accessible ISL media, versioned learning evidence, reliable backups, auditability, reproducible research and privacy safeguards.

## MVP
Identity, organisations, roles, classes, accessible story reading, ISL media, writing, assignments, rubric assessment, progress reporting, minimum governance and controlled research pilot foundations.

## Acceptance
No cross-tenant access; student writing preserved; assessment and content versions immutable after publication; research approval and eligibility enforced; no audio-only essential workflow.
